3. Strategic Note for Sonus
The "Miss" & "Mister" Nuance: In modern Mandarin, 
小姐
 (Miss) can be sensitive in certain contexts, but in a classroom/Band 1 setting, it is the standard formal address. Tagging it as polite helps the user understand it is a formal title.
The "Play" Distinction: The word 
玩
 (to play) almost always takes the 
儿
 suffix in Band 1 (
玩儿
). I have corrected the "Child" example to reflect this specific linguistic quirk.
Health & Politeness: In the "Routine" unit, phrases about health (
身体
) are often used as polite small talk. Even though we removed "Take care," saying "He is in good health" is a very common, simple way to practice this vocabulary.

3. Strategic Note for Sonus
The "Color" Rule: While beginners often want to learn colors, the HSK 3.0 Band 1 only includes the base adjectives like 
白
 (White) and 
黑
 (Black) (if present). The word for "color" (
颜
色
) and the suffix 
色
 are Level 2. In Sonus, describing something as "White Clothes" (
白衣服
) is a perfect way to stay within the rules.
Measure Word Simplification: The measure word 
个
 is the "universal" savior for Band 1. Since specific measure words like 
件
 (for clothes/matters) and 
首
 (for songs) are missing, using 
个
 or 
这
个
 is the most accurate way to stay in-band.
Replacing "Problem": As established, 
问题
 is Band 2. In your "Opinions" unit, replacing it with 
事
 (Matter/Thing) or 
话
 (Words) allows the user to express the same sentiment ("I have something else to do" or "What you said is wrong") with words they 
actually know
.

3. Summary of "Sonus" Language Logic
The "Polite" Particle: The word 
吧
 (
ba
) is a key social tool for beginners. Tagging it as polite helps the user understand it softens the tone of a command, making "Go" into "Let's go".
The "Question" Filter: By removing 
问题
 (Question/Problem) from the examples, we prevent the user from feeling stuck on a word that isn't in their Band 1 list.
Mouth-Feel & Speed: For the word 
慢
 (Slow), the example "Please speak a bit slower" is the single most important phrase for a real-life beginner. It uses only Band 1 words and provides immediate utility.
