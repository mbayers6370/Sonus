The Four Tones
Hear it · See it · Say it
1
First Tone
High Level ─
ā
Stay high and flat. Like holding a musical note. Think of saying “Ahhh” at the doctor — level, sustained, and high. It’s the easiest to identify because it doesn’t move.

Second Tone
Rising ╱

Third Tone
Low / Dip ˇ
ǎ
Dip, then lift. It drops low, then comes back up. In fast speech it often becomes a low “half-third” unless it’s stressed.
